Lasagna recipe

Home

Description

Lasagna is a layered pasta dish made by combining sheets of pasta with ingredients such as seasoned meat or vegetables, rich tomato sauce, creamy béchamel or ricotta mixture, and cheese. The layers are stacked repeatedly and baked until the flavors blend together and the top becomes golden and bubbly. It is a hearty, flavorful meal known for its rich texture and is popular in many cuisines, especially Italian-style cooking.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground Beef
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Olive Oil
  • Eggs
  • Salt
  • Black Pepper
  • Oregano herbs

Steps

  1. Prepare the ingredients Gather the lasagna sheets, meat or vegetables, tomato sauce, cheese, and seasonings.
  2. Cook the meat filling Heat oil in a pan and cook onions and garlic, then add the ground meat and cook until browned.
  3. Add the sauce Stir in tomato sauce, tomato paste, and seasonings, then simmer for a few minutes.
  4. Prepare the cheese mixture Mix ingredients such as Ricotta cheese, egg, and herbs if using a cheese filling.
  5. Cook the pasta sheets (if needed) Boil the Lasagna noodles according to package instructions if they are not oven-ready.
  6. Layer the lasagna Spread a thin layer of sauce in a baking dish, then add layers of pasta, meat sauce, cheese mixture, and shredded cheese. Repeat the layers.
  7. Add the top layer Finish with sauce and a generous layer of cheese on top.
  8. Bake Place the lasagna in a preheated oven and bake until fully cooked and the cheese becomes melted and golden.
  9. Rest and serve Allow the lasagna to rest for a few minutes before cutting and serving.
