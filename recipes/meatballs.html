Meatballs recipe

Home

Description

A meatballs recipe is a dish made by combining ground meat with ingredients such as breadcrumbs, eggs, herbs, spices, and seasonings, then shaping the mixture into small round balls. The meatballs are typically fried, baked, or simmered in a sauce until fully cooked. They can be served with pasta, rice, bread, or vegetables and are valued for their rich flavor, versatility, and ability to adapt to different cuisines and seasonings.

Ingredients

  • Ground Beef
  • Bread crumbs
  • Eggs
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Milk
  • Salt/li>
  • Black Pepper
  • Oregano herbs
  • Olive Oil

Steps

  1. Prepare the ingredients Gather the ground meat, breadcrumbs, egg, onions, garlic, herbs, and seasonings.
  2. Mix the ingredients Combine the Ground beef (or other ground meat) with breadcrumbs, egg, minced garlic, chopped onion, salt, pepper, and herbs in a bowl.
  3. Mix thoroughly Stir or gently combine the ingredients until evenly mixed without overworking the meat.
  4. Shape the meatballs Roll the mixture into small, evenly sized balls using your hands.
  5. Heat the pan or oven Add a little oil to a pan or preheat the oven if baking.
  6. Cook the meatballs Fry, bake, or simmer the meatballs until they are browned and fully cooked inside.
  7. Add sauce (optional) Place the meatballs in Tomato sauce or another preferred sauce and simmer for additional flavor.
  8. Serve Serve hot with pasta, rice, bread, or vegetables.
